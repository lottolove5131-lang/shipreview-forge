# Privacy Policy — Shipment Schedule Change Review for Jira

- The app reads, on demand, the Due date, change history and comments of the Jira issue being viewed (scope `read:jira-work`), using the permissions of the person viewing it (`asUser`).
- It does not store, copy, cache or export any data. Nothing is sent outside Atlassian's platform.
- It collects no analytics and no personal data.
- Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/lottolove5131-lang/shipreview-forge/issues
